ZENITH · THE HOME SCREEN

iOS 26, with a Zenith twist

Try it on the phone: move your mouse over it (that's you tilting it), press an icon, tap an empty bit of wallpaper, drag the page sideways, tap Search. Esc closes search.

Widgets
Wallpaper

The twists: tick the ones you want

1 · Light that moves with youThe glass catches the light as you tilt the phone, and the icons float a hair above the wallpaper. Move your mouse over the phone.
2 · Glass made from your wallpaperThe dock, Search and glass widgets take on your wallpaper's own colour, with a soft glow of it underneath. Change the wallpaper and the glass follows. Switch the wallpaper above.
3 · Liquid SearchThe pill stretches into the search bar instead of fading in, and while you swipe it turns into the page dots. Tap Search, or drag the page.
4 · A drop on the glassTap the wallpaper and a ring of light runs out through every pane, like a drop in water. Tap an empty spot.
5 · Jelly iconsPress an icon and it squishes and lights up from under your finger, then springs back. Press and hold an icon.

On the phone the glass is Zenith's own Liquid Glass: it bends the real wallpaper at its edges, which a web page can only blur.
